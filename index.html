Why it exists

A business is a hundred small handovers a day.

Something arrives at your door. It is counted, moved, worked on, sold, delivered or written off. A payment lands, or someone says it did. Every one of those is a moment where goods, services or money change hands, and every one of them is invisible until the figures are short at the end of the month.

Relynked puts a name and a time on every one of them, so a gap belongs to one handover between two people rather than to a total nobody can unpick.

A lounge

A crate leaves the store, the bar signs for it, a bottle is verified onto a table.

A supermarket

A pallet is received, the shelf is stocked, the till sells it, the count agrees.

A hotel

Linen is issued, a room is turned over, the housekeeper signs, the floor reconciles.
