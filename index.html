Why it exists

A venue is a hundred small handovers a night.

A bottle leaves the store. A plate leaves the pass. A transfer lands, or doesn't. Each one is a moment where money or stock changes hands, and each one is invisible until the count is wrong at the end of the month.

Relynked puts a name and a time on every one of them, so a gap belongs to one handover between two people rather than to a total nobody can unpick.
