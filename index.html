Why it exists

A business is a hundred small handovers a day.

Something arrives at your door. It is counted, moved, worked on, sold, delivered or written off. A payment lands, or someone says it did. Every one of those is a moment where goods, services or money change hands, and every one of them is invisible until the figures are short at the end of the month.

Relynked puts a name and a time on every one of them, so a gap belongs to one handover between two people rather than to a total nobody can unpick.

A lounge

A crate leaves the store, the bar signs for it, a bottle is verified onto a table.

A supermarket

A pallet is received, the shelf is stocked, the till sells it, the count agrees.

A hotel

Linen is issued, a room is turned over, the housekeeper signs, the floor reconciles.

How it works

Things move. So does responsibility.

Every handover is signed by both sides at the same moment.

Built for Nigeria

Designed around how business here actually runs.

Not a foreign system with Naira signs pasted on.

Every device saves locally first. The record catches up when the generator does. Nobody waits for a spinner mid-shift.

A screenshot proves nothing. The bill stays pending until a named person opens the bank app and confirms the credit.

Say what came in, in plain words. "From Mile 12: 5 crates tomatoes 24k each." It becomes the receipt.

Where you produce in batches, the system shows what is outstanding across every order at once, so the person making it knows whether to start another run.

Credit goes to a registered customer with a code and a standing. Nobody opens an account for the man in the blue shirt.

Personal and transaction data is stored in-country. We supply and set up the devices, locked to the job, so nothing leaves through a browser.
