Getting started

Relynked is a business management system for Nigerian businesses. It keeps what you sell and make, your stock, your staff, your money and your conversations with customers in one place.

Remy is the assistant inside Relynked. In the app, Ask Remy is where you tell Remy what you want done. On WhatsApp, Remy answers your customers on your business's behalf. Remy comes with every Relynked plan. It is not a separate app and has no separate price.

Relynked is built for restaurants, bars, bakeries, shops, supermarkets, barbers, salons and more. The full list is on our Who it is for page.

Sign up at app.relynked.com. If you would like to see it first, call 0916 221 6711 and we will come to you for a forty-minute walkthrough, on the devices you already have and with your own products and people. There is no commitment.

Relynked runs on the phone or computer you already have, at app.relynked.com. On the Venue plan our engineers set up your own devices for your business, and only suggest new ones if yours cannot do the job.

Remy and WhatsApp

Remy answers your customers on WhatsApp, day and night, on your business's behalf. It tells them what you sell and your prices, and takes their orders and bookings. You keep your prices and details up to date in Relynked, and Remy answers from them.

Remy only gives prices, stock, opening hours and payment details that you have set in Relynked, and asks the customer to confirm before it places an order. Like any AI, it can still get something wrong, so check how Remy is set up before you let it answer on its own. Your business stays responsible for what is said to your customers.

Straight away when a customer asks for a person or a call, complains, asks about a late order, or says they have already sent money. For anything else Remy offers first. It does not pass on greetings, typos or small talk.

Remy works on a separate WhatsApp connection that belongs to the business. It does not touch, read or connect to your personal WhatsApp, or to any of your staff's.

Remy only messages a customer who messaged your business first, or who agreed to be contacted, in line with WhatsApp's own rules for businesses.

In Ask Remy you say what you want in plain words, for example to change a price or to record stock that came in. Remy shows you the change first, and nothing changes until you confirm it. Remy can only do what you could do yourself, and each change is recorded against the person who asked for it.

The AI behind Remy is sent only what it needs to answer: the message, and the figures it refers to. Your business's records are never sent in full, and our contract with the AI provider does not allow it to use what we send to train its general models. Section 8 of our privacy policy has the detail.

Your team

The owner adds people, chooses each person's role and site, and can remove them. A member of staff sees only what their role needs, and cannot give themselves a bigger role or add other people.

Their name, phone number, email, sign in details and photo are erased straight away. What they did in the business, such as an order they took, stays in your records under “Deleted user”.

Your data and privacy

Your business records are stored in Nigeria. WhatsApp messages and Ask Remy requests also pass through providers outside Nigeria, because that is the only way those two features can work. Section 6 of our privacy policy explains it.

Each business's data is kept to that business alone. Another business cannot see your products, sales or conversations. This is built into the system, not only written in a policy.

Relynked does not sell data to anyone, and does not build advertising profiles of your business or your customers.

Cancelling does not delete your data, and neither does paying late. It is kept as our privacy policy describes. To have it erased, delete your account.

Plans and payment

Three: Starter, Standard and Venue. Starter and Standard can be paid monthly or yearly, and a yearly plan costs 10 months' price for 12 months. Venue is agreed with you after a walkthrough. What each plan includes and costs is on our pricing page. All prices are in naira.

Subscriptions are not sold inside the Relynked app on Android or iPhone. They are paid outside the app. Today each business pays by bank transfer into its own Relynked account number, which it gets when it signs up, and the payment is credited to its subscription automatically.

Relynked charges one flat monthly or yearly fee, with no percentage of your sales and no charge per transaction. When a customer pays you by card or transfer, the payment company that handles it charges its own fee. That fee is separate from Relynked.

Money your customers pay goes straight to your business through a licensed payment company. Relynked never holds it, moves it or refunds it. Refunds of those payments are between you and your customer.

We remind you 3 days before your renewal date. If the payment does not arrive, you have 7 days' grace with everything working, then 7 more days with warnings. On day 15 Remy pauses on WhatsApp until you pay. Nothing is deleted, and everything starts again as soon as the payment lands.
