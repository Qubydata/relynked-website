Two kinds of tester

Some run a test business. Others shop from it.

You do not choose your role. We place people so every test business has customers messaging it, and nobody is left talking to an empty shop.

Business tester

  • Ask Remy at least 5 things a day: sales, a new price, a deal, a report.

Customer tester

Up to ₦5,500 for 5 days, plus bonuses

30 to 45 minutes a day, any time, best in two sittings.

Questions

Before you apply.

Will other testers see my phone number?

No. Customer testers reach each test business with a short code sent to Relynked's own WhatsApp number, like "code ABC2". Nobody gets anyone's phone number, and nobody sees yours.

What data do I use?

Test data only. Make up your business, your products and your orders. Never use a real customer's details, a real card, or anyone's real bank details. No real money moves unless we tell you so in writing. Your screenshots and reports stay with Relynked.

Who picks the testers?

Relynked does, with our testing partner. Business tester places go first to people who run or have run a business and do well on the short quiz. Then we fill customer tester places so every test business has customers, and some of them chat only in Pidgin. Everyone else goes on the waiting list for the next sprint.

When will I hear back?

We email the people we pick within a week of sign up closing. If you are not picked this time, you stay on the waiting list and we may email you for a later sprint.

Do I have to write in Pidgin?

Only if we ask you to. Some customer testers chat fully in Pidgin, text and voice notes, for the whole sprint, because many real customers do. Tell us honestly how comfortable you are, so we place you well.

What phone do I need?

Any Android phone or iPhone with WhatsApp and a working camera. Business testers also install the Relynked app; we send the link when you are picked.

How and when am I paid?

Daily. Each active day is paid the next morning, straight to your own bank account, by our testing partner. Bonuses are paid at the end of the sprint, while the bonus pool lasts. Relynked itself handles no money.
