Long · 134 words

Relynked Ltd is a software company based in Benin City, Nigeria, building management systems for businesses that move goods, money and people. Its core product, Relynked Business, records every handover inside an operation, from the moment stock arrives to the moment it is sold, and attaches a named person and a timestamp to each one. A measurement engine then compares what the records say should have happened against what actually did, and surfaces the gaps.

A second product, Remy, answers a business's customers on WhatsApp. Both are designed around Nigerian conditions: unreliable power, bank transfers that may never land, and market buying that leaves no paper trail. Relynked began with bars, lounges and restaurants and is extending into retail, pharmacy, hotels and other sectors.

The story

Why it exists.

Most businesses in Nigeria discover a problem the same way: the figures are short at the end of the month, and nobody can say where or when. Not because people are dishonest, but because a hundred small handovers happen every day and almost none of them are recorded.

"A gap should belong to one handover between two people, not to a total nobody can unpick."

Relynked's answer is to make the recording invisible. A waiter takes an order, a storekeeper hands over a crate, a bartender checks a bottle. Each of those is already happening. Relynked simply makes sure each one carries a name and a time, and then does the arithmetic nobody has time to do by hand.

Angles worth exploring

Software built for power cuts. Every device saves locally before anything reaches a server, because assuming stable electricity is assuming a different country.

A transfer is not money until it lands. Relynked refuses to treat a payment screenshot as proof, which is a deliberate stance on a problem every Nigerian merchant recognises.

Counterfeit goods, not just theft. Fake product entering a legitimate business is a larger and less-discussed loss than staff theft, and it is designed for directly.

Data that stays in-country. Business records are held in Nigeria, and the company is explicit about the narrow exceptions.
