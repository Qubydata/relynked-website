How it works: goods move, services get done, and so does responsibility.

Each step is recorded by the person who did it, with a name and a time. Stock becomes the receiver's when the receiver accepts it, not when the giver says it left.

01
ReceiveWhat arrives is recorded as it arrives. Say it in plain words, like "5 crates tomatoes 24k each", and that record is the receipt.
02
RequestWhoever needs it asks for it. The kitchen asks the store for tomorrow's tomatoes. One list, many lines.
03
Hand overThe giver brings it. The receiver counts it and accepts it. Responsibility moves at that moment, not before.
04
DeliverThe work gets done and reaches the customer, with every stage visible to whoever is waiting on it.
05
ReconcileBoth ends count blind, and each answers to its own record.
Requested

What was asked for, recorded by whoever asked.

Issued

What actually left, recorded by whoever released it.

Received

What actually arrived, recorded by whoever received it. When these three differ, the gap has names and a time on it.

A count that can see the answer is not a count.

There are two places where the numbers must agree, and both count blind. You enter what is actually there before the system shows what it expected. Then the gaps appear, together, each one a permanent line with a name on it.

The store

received − issued = counted

What came in through your door, less what was handed on to the people who use it, should be what is left in the store. Anything else is a gap, and it has a name on it.

Where it is used

received − used − waste = counted

What was received, less what the system expected to be used for what you sold, less what was written off, should be what is left at close. Anything else is a gap, and it has a name on it.
