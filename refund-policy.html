Refund policy: your money, in plain terms.

What happens to money you send to Relynked, what you can always have back, what we keep, and how to cancel.

What this covers

This policy covers money a business pays to Relynked Ltd (RC 9837720) for Relynked, including Remy, the assistant inside it. Plans and prices are on our pricing page. This policy forms part of our terms of service. It does not cover money your customers pay you: that goes straight to your business and Relynked never holds it.

Your wallet

Money you send to your Relynked account number goes into your Relynked wallet. Your wallet is prepaid credit for Relynked's own services: your plan, extra screens and WhatsApp messages. It is not a bank account. You cannot send money from it to anyone else, and it earns no interest.

Money in your wallet is yours until it is used

  • You can ask for any unused money back at any time, for any reason. That includes money you sent ahead for months that have not started yet.
  • We send it back to the bank account it came from within 7 working days. We do not charge a fee and there is no minimum.
  • If that account can no longer receive money, we send it to a bank account in your business's or your own name after you confirm it with your PIN.
  • Wallet money never expires. If you close your account, we send back what is left without you needing to ask.

How your plan is paid

When you sign up, you choose whether to pay your plan from your wallet when it is due. Nothing is chosen for you, and you can change your answer at any time in the app or by asking Remy.

  • If you choose to pay from your wallet, we take the price of one period the day before your paid time runs out. We never take part of a plan, and we never take your wallet below zero.
  • If your plan is already overdue, we take it as soon as your wallet holds enough, so Remy comes back on.
  • If you choose to pay yourself, we remind you before your plan ends and you tap Pay for plan when you are ready. Your wallet money stays yours either way.
  • We never pause a business whose wallet holds the price of its plan while paying from the wallet is switched on.

Paying too much or too little

If you send more than your plan costs, the extra stays in your wallet for your next period, screens or messages, and you can ask for it back at any time. We never turn extra money into a longer plan unless you ask. If you send less, nothing is taken; your money waits in your wallet and we tell you how much more is needed.

Wallet limit

Your wallet can hold up to 12 months of your plan. If a payment would take it above that, we add what fits and send the rest back to the account it came from within 7 working days, and we tell you we have done so.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel at any time, in the app or by asking Remy. It takes no more steps than signing up did. You can also email or call us.
  • There is no cancellation fee and no minimum term.
  • Your plan keeps working until the end of the period already paid, and then stops. Any money still in your wallet is yours to take back.
  • Your data is not deleted when you cancel. It is kept and handled as described in our privacy policy.

What we keep

We keep only money for things you have already had. We show these before you pay or sign up:

  • Time already used. Days, weeks or months of your plan that have gone by.
  • A month that has started, if you change your mind. Once a monthly period has started, we do not refund it on a change of mind, because it is ready for you to use until its last day. This does not apply when the fault is ours (see If we let you down).
  • Messages already sent. WhatsApp messages your business has already sent.

Your first payment. If you cancel within 7 days of your very first payment for a plan, we refund that payment in full, even though the month has started.

Yearly plans

You can cancel a yearly plan at any time. We refund what you paid, minus the months you have used, charged at the normal monthly price for your plan. A month you have started counts as used.

An example. You pay ₦200,000 for a Basic yearly plan and cancel in month 3. Three months at ₦20,000 is ₦60,000, so we refund ₦140,000.

WhatsApp messages

Sending WhatsApp messages is a Relynked service with its own rates, shown in the app before it is switched on. When we charge for messages from your wallet, we take them once a day for the day before. We cannot refund a message once it has been sent, unless we charged it by mistake.

Prices

  • A period you have already paid for keeps the price you paid. A yearly plan keeps its price until the year ends.
  • We give you at least 30 days' notice before any price change applies to your account.
  • Money already in your wallet pays at the new price only after that notice. If you would rather not, you can take it back instead, or cancel before the change.

Duplicate or mistaken payments

If you pay twice, or send money to your Relynked account number by mistake, it is in your wallet and you can ask for it back in full, as above.

If we let you down

If Relynked or Remy does not work for a meaningful time because of a fault on our side, we add the lost time to your plan or refund a fair part of what you paid for that period, whichever you prefer.

Your customers' payments

Relynked never holds or refunds money that customers pay to a business. When a customer pays a business for an order, that money goes directly to the business through a licensed payment company. It does not pass through Relynked.

Any refund of a customer's payment is between the customer and the business. A customer who wants their money back should contact the business they paid.

How to ask for a refund

  1. In the app, open Plan and tap Ask for a refund, or ask Remy. You can also email hello@relynked.com or call 0916 221 6711.
  2. We confirm we have your request within 2 working days.
  3. We send approved refunds to the account the money came from within 7 working days, in Nigerian naira, with no fee.
  4. Every refund comes with a written confirmation in the app.

If you are not happy with our answer

Tell us and we will look at it again within 15 working days. You can also contact the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission.
