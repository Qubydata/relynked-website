Why it exists

A venue is a hundred small handovers a night.

A bottle leaves the store. A plate leaves the pass. A transfer lands, or doesn't. Each one is a moment where money or stock changes hands, and each one is invisible until the count is wrong at the end of the month.

Relynked puts a name and a time on every one of them, so a gap belongs to one handover between two people rather than to a total nobody can unpick.

How it works

Stock moves. So does responsibility.

Every handover is signed by both sides at the same moment. Custody moves when the receiver approves it, not when the giver says it left.

DeliverStore carries it over. Chef counts it in front of them and signs.

Bottle verification

An outside bottle cannot be sold as yours.

Premium stock gets a serial the day it enters the store. At the bar, the bartender scans and the screen finds the order on its own. No extra taps. Four honest outcomes, not two.

VerifiedOurs, issued today, not served before. Added to the table.
Not on our recordDid not come through the store. Stops the line, calls a supervisor.
Already servedScanned onto another table earlier tonight.
Verified, nobody ordered itThe bottle is fine. Only the paperwork is missing.

It flags. It never accuses. A supervisor decides.

Two reconciliation points

A count that can see the answer is not a count.

Both the store and the kitchen count blind. You enter what is on the shelf before the system shows what it expected. Then the gaps appear, together, each one a permanent line with a name on it.

The store

intake − issued = counted

What came in through the door, less what was delivered to the kitchen and bar, should be what is on the shelf. Anything else is a gap the storekeeper signs for.

The kitchen

received − sold − waste = counted

What was signed for, less what the recipes say was used and what was logged as waste, should be what is left at close. Anything else is a gap the chef signs for.

Built for Nigeria

Designed around the night as it actually is.

Not a Western system with Naira signs pasted on.

Keeps working through a power cut

Every device saves locally first. The record catches up when the generator does. Nobody waits for a spinner mid-service.

A transfer is not money until it lands

A screenshot proves nothing. The bill stays pending until a named person opens the bank app and confirms the credit.

Market buying with no paper

Say what came in, in plain words. "From Mile 12: 5 crates tomatoes 24k each." It becomes the receipt.

Cooking in pots, not plates

The kitchen sees how many jollof are on order right now, so the chef knows whether to start another pot.

A tab is a person, never a table

Credit goes to a registered customer with a code and a standing. Nobody opens a tab for the man in the blue shirt.

Your data stays in Nigeria

Personal and transaction data is stored in-country. We supply the devices, locked to the job, so nothing leaves through a browser.
