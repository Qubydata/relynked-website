Relynked, in plain facts.

Short answers about what Relynked is, who it is for, what it costs and what it does not do. Written to be quoted. Last updated 13 October 2026.

What is Relynked?

Relynked is a business app for small businesses in Nigeria. It keeps what you sell, your stock, your staff, your orders and your payments in one place, and it answers your customers on WhatsApp through Remy, the assistant inside it.

Relynked is made by Relynked Ltd, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 9837720) and based in Benin City, Edo State.

What problem does it solve?

A small business usually runs on one phone. Customers message at 11pm and go elsewhere if nobody answers. A payment screenshot says successful, the goods leave, and the money is not in the account. Stock lives in someone's head. Staff share the owner's phone or one password, so when a price changes or an order goes missing, nobody did it.

Relynked answers those customers, holds each order until the money has really landed, keeps a count for every item, and gives every staff member their own sign-in, so each change has a name and a time.

Who is it for?

Small businesses in Nigeria that sell products or book services: shops, supermarkets, clothing and shoe sellers, phone and gadget shops, bakeries, grills, fast food spots, bukas, restaurants, bars and lounges, hair salons, barbers, makeup artists, nail and lash technicians, spas and car washes. The full list is on the products page. If your kind of business is not listed, you can choose “Something else” when you sign up.

What is Remy?

Remy is the assistant inside Relynked. It is not a separate app. Remy answers your customers on your business WhatsApp number, day and night, in English or Pidgin. It gives only the prices and stock you have entered, reads each order back, and waits for the customer to say yes. When a customer needs a person, Remy hands the chat to you and alerts you.

How much does Relynked cost?

Basic is ₦20,000 a month. Standard is ₦40,000 a month. Enterprise starts from ₦150,000 a month and the price is agreed after a call. Paying quarterly costs ₦57,000 for Basic and ₦114,000 for Standard. Paying yearly costs ₦200,000 for Basic and ₦400,000 for Standard. Prices are in Nigerian naira. See the pricing page for the full list.

Relynked charges no percentage of your sales and no fee per transaction.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Relynked is free for with no card. When the trial ends you pick a plan and pay in the app.

What does each plan include?

Staff are unlimited on every plan. The plan only counts the people who answer customers on your WhatsApp number or use Remy: the owner plus 2 on Basic, the owner plus 5 on Standard, and the owner plus 10 on Enterprise.

Basic includes up to 500 customer chats a month. Standard includes up to 1,000 customer chats a month and 1 screen at your place. Enterprise adds more screens and chats, till, kitchen, bar and store screens on the devices you have, blind stock counts, and several branches under one owner.

How do I start?

Go to app.relynked.com and sign up. No card is needed. Add what you sell, and Remy can answer your next customer on WhatsApp.

How is Relynked different?

A payment screenshot is not treated as money. Remy sends your locked payment details, and the order waits until someone you trust sees the money in your bank.

Every change has a name and a time, because each staff member signs in as themselves and sees only what their job needs.

Stock is kept per item. When something finishes, Remy stops offering it to customers.

It is built around how business runs in Nigeria: prices in naira, customers who write in English or Pidgin, and transfers that may not have landed yet.

What does Relynked not do?

Relynked does not hold your customers' money. Customers pay your business through a licensed payment company, and Relynked never takes a cut of it.

Relynked does not pay Meta's charges for WhatsApp messages. Meta charges for some messages, and that charge is separate from your plan.

Relynked is not made by WhatsApp or Meta, and is not a bank.

Only the till and waiter screens keep taking orders when the network drops. The rest of the app needs an internet connection.
