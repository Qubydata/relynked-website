Acceptance

By creating an account, or by using Relynked Business or Remy on behalf of a business, that business accepts these terms. If someone is accepting on behalf of a business, they are confirming they have the authority to do so.

The services

Relynked Ltd (RC 9837720) operates two products. Relynked Business is the operations and measurement system a venue runs day to day. Remy is the WhatsApp assistant that answers customer messages and, when directed by an owner, carries out specific authorised actions inside a business's own account.

Accounts

A business owner is responsible for the staff accounts created under their business, and for making sure the information attached to those accounts is accurate. Login credentials should not be shared between staff members, since Relynked Business ties a given action, a price change, a stock adjustment, a reconciliation note, back to the account that made it, and a shared login breaks that trail. An owner is responsible for what happens under an account until they remove it or report it compromised.

Owner accounts

  • Can create and remove staff accounts, assign roles, and use Ask Remy to direct changes across the business.
  • Are the account Relynked contacts for billing, policy updates, and account-level decisions.

Staff accounts

  • Are scoped to the role and venue an owner assigns them.
  • Cannot grant themselves a wider role or create further accounts.

Acceptable use

The service may not be used for anything unlawful, to send unsolicited bulk messages, or in a way that breaches WhatsApp's own Business Messaging Policy, since Remy operates a business's WhatsApp connection on its behalf and that connection depends on staying within those rules. A business is responsible for making sure it only messages customers who have messaged it first or have otherwise given consent to be contacted. A business also agrees not to use Ask Remy to attempt an action it does not have the authority to make, and not to attempt to access another business's data through the platform.

Fees are billed as described at signup for the plan a business chooses, in Nigerian naira unless stated otherwise, and a business will be told in advance of any change before it takes effect on their account.
