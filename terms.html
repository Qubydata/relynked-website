Agreeing to these terms

A business agrees to these terms when its owner taps to accept them at sign up, after being given the chance to read them. Before the first payment, we also show, on their own and in plain words: the price, how the plan renews, what can be refunded and what cannot, and how to cancel. Nothing is ticked for you. Whoever accepts on behalf of a business confirms they have the authority to do so.

We keep a record of which version of these terms and of our refund policy each business accepted, when, and by which owner, together with the owner's choice about paying the plan from the wallet.

The service

Relynked Ltd (RC 9837720) provides Relynked, the system a business runs day to day: what it sells, its stock, its staff, its money and its chats with customers. Remy is the assistant inside Relynked. It answers customer messages on WhatsApp and, when an owner directs it, carries out specific authorised actions inside the business's own account.

Accounts

A business owner is responsible for the staff accounts created under their business, and for making sure the information attached to those accounts is accurate. Login details should not be shared between staff members, since Relynked ties each action (a price change, a stock adjustment, a reconciliation note) back to the account that made it, and a shared login breaks that trail. An owner is responsible for what happens under an account until they remove it or report it compromised.

Owner accounts

  • Can create and remove staff accounts, assign roles, and use Ask Remy to direct changes across the business.
  • Are the accounts Relynked contacts for billing, policy updates, and account level decisions.

Staff accounts

  • Are limited to the role and venue an owner assigns them.
  • Cannot grant themselves a wider role or create further accounts.

Acceptable use

The service may not be used for anything unlawful, to send unsolicited bulk messages, or in a way that breaches WhatsApp's own Business Messaging Policy, since Remy operates a business's WhatsApp connection on its behalf and that connection depends on staying within those rules. A business is responsible for making sure it only messages customers who have messaged it first or have otherwise agreed to be contacted. A business also agrees not to use Ask Remy to attempt an action it does not have the authority to make, and not to attempt to access another business's data through the platform.

Voice notes and sensitive information. Remy can understand voice notes by turning them into text with an AI provider before any of our own checks run on the words. For that reason, we advise businesses and their customers not to send passwords, PINs, one-time codes, card numbers or other sensitive information as voice notes through Relynked. Typed messages are screened for codes before any AI sees them; voice notes cannot be screened until they have been turned into text.

Fees and billing

Current plans and prices are listed on the pricing page, in Nigerian naira unless stated otherwise. Suspending an account for non-payment does not remove a business's data, which is kept for the period described in the privacy policy in case the account is reinstated.

The wallet

  • Each business gets its own Relynked account number. Money sent to it by bank transfer goes into the business's Relynked wallet.
  • The wallet is prepaid credit for Relynked's own services: the plan, extra screens and WhatsApp messages. It is not a bank account. Money in it cannot be sent to anyone else, and it earns no interest.
  • Unused wallet money belongs to the business. It can be asked back at any time and is returned to the account it came from within 7 working days, with no fee, as set out in the refund policy. It never expires.
  • A wallet can hold up to 12 months of the business's plan. Anything above that is sent back to the account it came from.

WhatsApp messages

Sending WhatsApp messages is a Relynked service with its own rates, shown in the app before it is switched on. Messages already sent are not refunded, unless we charged them by mistake.

Subscriptions, missed payments and price changes

Billing

  • Relynked is paid for by subscription, monthly or yearly, at the prices on the pricing page. A yearly plan costs the same as 10 months of the monthly price.
  • At sign up, the owner chooses whether the plan is paid from the wallet when it is due. Nothing is chosen for them, and they can change the choice at any time in the app or by asking Remy.
  • If the plan is paid from the wallet, we take the price of one period the day before paid time runs out, and only when the wallet holds the full price. If the plan is overdue, we take it as soon as the wallet holds enough.
  • If the owner chooses to pay themselves, the plan renews only when they tap Pay for plan.
  • Extra money is never turned into a longer plan unless the owner asks.
  • Relynked takes no percentage of a business's sales and charges no fee per transaction. Fees charged by a payment company on payments from a business's customers are separate and are charged by that company.

Missed payments

  • We send a reminder 3 days before a subscription renews.
  • If payment is not received on the renewal date, the business has 7 days' grace with everything working as normal.
  • For the 7 days after that, the service keeps working and we send warnings.
  • On day 15 after the renewal date, the WhatsApp assistant pauses until the payment is made. It starts again once payment is received.
  • When a free trial ends without a plan being paid, the business has 3 days' grace, and then the WhatsApp assistant pauses until a plan is paid.
  • We never pause a business whose wallet holds the price of its plan while paying from the wallet is switched on.
  • Nothing is deleted because a payment is late.

Price changes

  • A period a business has already paid for keeps the price it paid. A yearly plan keeps its price until the year ends.
  • We give account owners at least 30 days' notice before any price change applies to their account.
  • Money already in the wallet pays at the new price only after that notice. A business that does not accept the new price can cancel before it applies and take its unused wallet money back.

Cancelling and refunds

A business can cancel at any time in the app or by asking Remy, with no more steps than signing up took, or by email or phone. There is no fee and no minimum term. The plan stays active until the end of the period already paid for. Refunds are set out in our refund policy, which forms part of these terms.

Data

What is collected, how it is used, and how to control it is set out in the privacy policy, which forms part of this agreement. A business keeps ownership of its own catalogue, transaction and customer data at all times. We hold it to provide the service, not to claim it as our own.

Ownership

Relynked Ltd owns Relynked, Remy, and the software behind them. Nothing in these terms transfers that ownership to a business using the service. A business keeps ownership of its own business data, as described above.

Availability

We aim to keep the service available and reliable, and we take reasonable steps toward that, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted access. Planned maintenance and unexpected issues can both cause short periods of downtime. If the service does not work for a meaningful time because of a fault on our side, we add the lost time to the plan or refund a fair part of what was paid for that period, as the business prefers.

Termination

A business may stop using the service at any time. We may suspend or close an account that breaches these terms, particularly the acceptable use section, or that puts other businesses' data at risk. If an account is closed for any reason, we send any unused wallet money back to the account it came from without being asked. Data is handled after termination as described in the privacy policy's retention section.

Nothing in these terms takes away any right a business has under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018, or limits our liability for gross negligence.

Complaints

A business can complain in the app, by asking Remy, by email or by phone. We acknowledge a complaint in writing within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 15 working days. If we need longer, we say why and by when. If a business is not happy with our answer, it can contact the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission.

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
