Anti-counterfeit

Fake goods do not get sold as yours.

Counterfeit and diverted goods cost Nigerian businesses more than theft does, and they arrive looking exactly like the real thing. Relynked checks anything of value against your own record at the moment somebody tries to sell it, in real time, before it reaches the customer. Anything that did not come through your business stops there and asks for a supervisor.

It flags. It never accuses. A person decides.
