You close at 10. At 11 the phone is still going: how much is this, do you deliver to Lekki, hello?? Reply late and the customer buys from the next shop. Reply to everyone and you never rest.

With Relynked: Remy, the assistant inside Relynked, answers your business WhatsApp day and night. It gives only your real prices and what you have, reads each order back, and waits for the customer to say yes. When a customer needs a person, Remy hands the chat to you and alerts you at once.

  • Prices and stock come from your own list, so Remy never makes up a price.
  • Every order gets its own number and is confirmed with a clear yes.
  • Change a price or mark something finished by telling Ask Remy in plain words.
  • When an item is finished, Remy offers the nearest thing and can message the customer when it is back.

A customer sends a screenshot that says Successful. Your sales girl sees it and releases the goods. At night the money is not in the account. Screenshots are easy to fake and easy to misread, and once the goods are gone, they are gone.

With Relynked: Remy sends your payment details itself, so nobody can swap the account number. A screenshot is not money. The order waits until someone you trust checks your bank and confirms what actually landed.

  • Your payment details are locked with a PIN, and a change waits before it takes effect.
  • If less than the order total landed, Relynked will not mark the order paid.
  • The customer is told the payment is confirmed only after that check.
  • Every order and what was paid for it sit together, with no notebooks to add up.

Stock lives in someone's head or on a sheet behind the door. A customer asks for an item, your staff says yes, then finds the shelf empty. Or you only learn that something finished last week when a customer complains.

With Relynked: each item keeps its own count, and you choose the level that counts as low. When something finishes, Remy stops offering it to customers.

  • Update stock by telling Ask Remy what came in, or edit it in the app.
  • Count what needs counting, like drinks by the bottle. For the rest, just mark available or finished.
  • Bars and restaurants on Relynked Floor get a store screen for deliveries and counts.
