How paying works

Simple to pay, fair when you're late.

No card machine to buy, no contract to sign, and nothing deleted if a month runs late.

Pay by bank transfer

Every business gets its own Relynked account number. Send your subscription to it by bank transfer and it is credited to your account automatically. Card or direct debit autopay will be offered as an option later.

Your sales stay yours

Money your customers pay goes straight to your business through a licensed payment company. Relynked never holds it and never takes a cut of it.

If a payment is missed

We remind you 3 days before renewal. After that you get 7 days' grace with everything working, then 7 more days with warnings. On day 15 the WhatsApp assistant pauses until you pay. Nothing is deleted.
